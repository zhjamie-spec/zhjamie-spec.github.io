Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This tool accesses only the Google Drive of its single owner, with the owner's permission, to copy the owner's own files.

  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any data with anyone.
  • It has no other users and no server of its own.
  • Data from Google APIs is used only to sync the owner's files and is not transferred to any third party.

The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
